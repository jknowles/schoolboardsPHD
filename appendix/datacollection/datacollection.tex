\section*{Introduction}

A major part of the effort associated with this dissertation involved the data collection. Collecting new data was vital to the success of this dissertation and this appendix attempts to capture the outline of this data collection effort. 

\section*{Strategy}

Three distinct sets of data were needed for this dissertation:
\begin{enumerate}
  \item School district finances
  \item Election results
  \item Student-level longitudinal test scores
\end{enumerate}

Each of these data presented unique data collection challenges requiring different strategies to collect and assemble the data for this purpose. 

\subsubsection*{School Finance Data}

The Department of Public Instruction maintains detailed records on the finances 
of all of the public school districts in the state. This data is reported and 
made publicly available through the School Finance Data Warehouse (SFDW) at DPI. 

Data sources:
\begin{itemize}
\item DPI: http://dpi.wi.gov/sfs/long\_data.html
\item DPI: http://www2.dpi.state.wi.us/sfsref/ref\_Home.aspx
\item DPI Finance Data Warehouse: http://www2.dpi.state.wi.us/sfsdw/Download.asp
\end{itemize}


\subsubsection*{Election Results}

Two types of election results were necessary for the analyses above. First, the ward-level results of statewide elections were necessary to understand the political climate within each school district. 

Second, and more difficultly, election results from Wisconsin school board elections were needed.
