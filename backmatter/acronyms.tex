This dissertation makes common use of the following abbreviations and jargon. 

\begin{myitemize}
\item \textsc{DPI} : the Wisconsin Department of Public Instruction
\item \textsc{MCD} : Minor Civil Division -- local government reporting unit
\item \textsc{VAP} : Voting Age Population
\item \textsc{GAB} : the Wisconsin Governmental Accountability Board
\item \textsc{WASB} : the Wisconsin Association of School Boards
\item \textsc{millrate} = the property tax rate set by local governments
\item \textsc{WCER} : Wisconsin Center for Education Research
\item \textsc{EQV} : Equalized property value, used for property tax valuation
\item \textsc{LEA} : Local education agency, a school district
\item \textsc{SEA} : State education agency, the DPI
\end{myitemize}
